About BMI Calculator

Estimate BMI from your weight and height. BMI is a general screening measure and is not a medical diagnosis.

How to use

Enter weight in kilograms and height in centimeters, then select Calculate.

How it works

BMI is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared.

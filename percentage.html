How to use the Percentage Calculator

What is X% of Y?

Enter the percentage and the number. For example, 20% of 850 gives 170.

X is what % of Y?

Enter the first number and the total number. For example, 495 is what percentage of 850? The answer is 58.24%.

Percentage Change

Enter the original value and the new value. The calculator will show the percentage increase or decrease.
